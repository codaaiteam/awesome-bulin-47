## What is the Bulin 47 / Dame Hombro trend?

The **Bulin 47** clip is one of the most-copied dembow moments of the year: an improvised freestyle performed over a speaker outside a restaurant in Madrid, with a crowd filming it on their phones. The signature **"dame hombro"** (give-me-shoulder) bounce, the charisma, and the lo-fi street energy made it instantly recognizable — and instantly remixable.

It spread the way the best memes do: it's simple, it's joyful, and it's *easy to copy*. That's why so many people now want to see **themselves** in the scene — same club, same crowd, same move.

## Who is Bulin 47?

Bulin 47 is a **Dominican dembow artist** who went viral for that improvised **Dame Hombro** freestyle. The moment — often linked with Kevin Wai — jumped from Dominican dembow circles to a global audience because the hook and the shoulder-bounce move are so easy to recognize and repeat.

## What does "Dame Hombro" mean?

"Dame hombro" literally means **"give me shoulder."** In the clip it's the playful shoulder-bounce move Bulin 47 does to the beat — now the signature gesture of the whole trend. People recreating the video lean into that same bounce.

## How to recreate the Bulin 47 video with AI

You don't need to learn the flow, dance, or video editing. The modern way to do it is a **two-step AI pipeline**:

1. **Clean portrait first.** Take one clear, front-facing photo of your face. Use an image model to turn it into a **clean 9:16 portrait** — natural lighting, no sunglasses, no costume, face clearly visible. (Sunglasses or heavy styling hide your face and ruin the "that's me!" payoff — keep it clean.)
2. **Character-replace into the scene.** Feed that portrait + a reference clip of the Bulin 47 scene into a **character-replacement / motion-transfer video model**. The model swaps the lead performer for *you* while keeping the crowd, the camera movement, the choreography, and the original audio.

The result is a vertical **9:16** video, ideally at **1080p Full HD**, with the original sound — ready to post.

### Tips that actually matter

- **One clear front-facing photo** beats a group shot or a profile angle every time.
- **Good, even lighting** on the face = a far more convincing result.
- **Keep it clean** at the portrait step — let the scene provide the vibe, not your selfie.
- **Vertical 9:16** matches TikTok/Reels natively; no cropping afterwards.
- **Label it as AI-generated** when you post, and only use photos you're allowed to use.
